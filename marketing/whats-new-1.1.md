# What's New in 1.1 — App Store copy

Paste into App Store Connect → version 1.1 → "What's New in This Version".

---

A whole new phase: STEAM.

Tap to burst into vapor — rise on your own updraft, drift with the tilt,
and float untouched over drains and grates… but you'll condense back to
water on a short fuse, and icicles end vapor instantly. Frozen solid?
Steam anyway — ice sublimates.

• 9 new levels (14–22), including an expert tier that tests everything
  you've learned
• Levels 11–13 redesigned — every late level now has exactly one
  intended route (find a cheat? Tell us. We'll be impressed, then fix it.)
• The droplet is now truly liquid: real refraction, glints, and a
  contact shadow
• Every diorama has its own mood — frost, boiler-light, storm, mist
• Redesigned drain grates, glassy carve channels with an ink meter, and
  a smarter menu with level thumbnails
• Breath lifts when your mic is available; touch-and-hold takes over
  when it isn't

Water splatters. Ice flies. Steam rises.
