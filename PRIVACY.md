# Privacy Policy — Drip Drop Dont Stop

Drip Drop Dont Stop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections of any kind.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.**
- **Microphone**: with your permission, the microphone is used solely to
  measure blowing intensity in real time for the blow-to-lift game mechanic.
  Audio is processed on-device and in the moment; it is never recorded,
  stored, or transmitted. Declining microphone access disables nothing —
  touch & hold is an equivalent input.
- **Scores** (per-level bests and best run) are stored only on your device
  and never leave it.

Questions: open an issue on this repository or contact dgperkins@gmail.com.
